Slip and Reclaim run in your browser on public RPC. That is fine for a preview. Long signature history, or a wallet with hundreds of token accounts, rate-limits the tab. Mail the address. We page the RPC here. You pay 12 USDC. The CSV comes back to the same inbox.

What you get

One job. One address. One file.

  • Wallet dump — native SOL, token accounts, signatures paged past the first 50
  • Empty-account list — every zero-balance ATA with lamports, for closing later
  • Not a holder snapshot of a huge mint. Public RPC will not return that cheaply. Do not ask.

Not tax advice. Public RPC is not a full archive. If the endpoint gives up, you get what it gave, plus a note. We never ask for a seed.
